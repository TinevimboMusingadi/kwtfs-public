# Data sources and licence register

Raw downloads go in `data/kwtfs/raw/`, which is gitignored and never redistributed. The loaders are in [kwtfs/data/sources.py](../../kwtfs/data/sources.py). Only **train** splits of public benchmarks are used, because Qwen3 has probably seen their test splits. Our own sealed test is carved out of this pool.

| source | HF id | licence (Hub tag) | split used | genre | redistribution | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bev-decision | `avbiswas/bev-decision` (config `default`) | unknown; mixed upstream sources, no blanket licence asserted | train | decision | IDs and scripts only | JEV-native: choice, yes/no and score questions over 18 domains. Labels come from upstream annotations, logs and code rules. One question is sampled per state, and states over 1,200 characters are dropped. |
| GSM8K | `openai/gsm8k` (`main`) | MIT | train | wordproblem | yes | Gold answer is the value after `####`. Step count is taken from `<<…>>` calculator annotations. |
| AQuA-RAT | `deepmind/aqua_rat` (`raw`) | Apache-2.0 | train | wordproblem | yes | Crowd-augmented and noisy. 2.4% fail the schema check (duplicate options or a repeated gold answer), and about 7% are near-duplicates. Its label error rate is audited in Phase 1.2. |
| ARC | `allenai/ai2_arc` (Easy and Challenge) | CC-BY-SA-4.0 | train | knowledge | yes, share-alike | AI2's page also says not to redistribute outside AI2. We release IDs only, to be safe. |
| OpenBookQA | `allenai/openbookqa` (`main`) | unknown | train | knowledge | IDs and scripts only | |
| CommonsenseQA | `tau/commonsense_qa` | MIT | train | knowledge | yes | 1.7% fail the schema check. |
| procedural | generated by `kwtfs/data/sources.py:procedural` | generated (ours) | — | compare, arith, rubric | yes | Exact gold answers. The difficulty knobs are digits, gap, steps and rubric depth. Yes/no gold answers and rubric scores are balanced by construction. |

Dropped: MathQA (`allenai/math_qa` is script-only and downloads from an external URL; AQuA covers the genre). GSM8K-Platinum and MMLU-Redux clean only *test* splits, which we don't use.
